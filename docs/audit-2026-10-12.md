# Code audit, 2026-10-12

A review of site-api and this repository, and what came of it. Every fix was checked the same way: a test or a
browser scenario that shows the problem on the old code, the change, the same check passing; deployed fixes were
checked on the live site too. Commits: site-api (`s-a`) and this repository (`site`).

## Left as they are, on purpose

- JavaScript is not bundled like the CSS: over HTTP/2 the files load in parallel, and one script's syntax error does
  not take the others down.
- "hosted on belomaxorka infrastructure" and the silkscreen labels stay English in both languages: they are labels
  printed on the hardware, like a badge.
- `Hub.Online` is used by the live stream's tests only; colours outside the tokens are decorative and do not change
  with the theme.

## Lamps and activity

The rack looked frozen: no lamp had an animation at rest, and the few blinks came from a visit, a guestbook entry or
a poll that brought new data (on the live site in 75 s: 5 WAN blinks, the disk lamp once, nothing on the neighbour
racks or the monitoring lamps). Every blink now comes from something the server really did, sent by site-api once a
second (`act`): requests served, sources fetched (a packet from the server to the unit that shows the source),
state files written (the disk lamp), Gatus checks run (their lamps, here and on the neighbour racks, with the real
spacing). New data refreshes its unit at once (`fresh`). The switch's SYS lamp stays steady, as on a healthy switch.

## Comments and docs

Every comment in this repository is in English now: 1032 lines were Russian (scripts, styles, templates, content
files, the tools). The Russian left is the site's own text (`i18n/ru.json`, the `ru:` values in `content/`); in
site-api it is what the owner reads (the Telegram bot, the admin page) and test data. Notes about who asked for
what, dates of decisions and rejected variants are gone. Comments that had gone stale were corrected on the way:
`/u/<unit>` (now `?open=`), "every fetch of a rack is a visit" (`X-Rack-Swap`), the hdd lamp, the neighbour racks
as "blanks and diodes", the `router` field of `racks.yaml` and `Rack.Router` in site-api's README. `docs/ideas.md`,
a diary of requests mostly marked done, is deleted: what was still true is in the READMEs (what changes without Go;
the ntfy buttons' signatures that do not expire).

Checked that only comments changed: esbuild's minified output of every script and stylesheet is identical before
and after (36 files), the content files parse to the same data (8), the Python tool's AST and site-api's Go AST
without comments are identical, the 32 rendered pages are byte-identical (the assets' `?v=` hashes masked: they
follow the bytes, comments included), and the diff of the preview card and the favicon touches comment lines only.

- The home rack's controls (screens, theme, sound, UID, RESET, the copy buttons, the UPS's power and self-test, the
  light switch, the breaker, the torch), the BIOS by the Konami code and by five taps with its three settings, POST
